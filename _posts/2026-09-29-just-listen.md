Explanations do not comfort. My head does not hurt. My heart does. Just sit with me. 

You do have to share my hurt to share my heart. Only be there while I go on this journey. Walk with me a while, even if your path diverges just over the horizon.

Do not tell me it was God's will or divine order or that cookies do or do not crumble or that omelettes require a certain measure of broken eggs. 

Do not tell me at all. Hear me.

Slavoj Žižek said "if you have reasons to love someone, you don't love them." Meet me in that place, where there are not reasons but experiences and journeys and care.

Meet me where love lives. I am waiting.
