Find me in the ashes, the dust, the cinders, amongst the husks, in the wilderness, but find me. You will want to see what happens next.

I am down, but not done. This is where I root and feed and grow. This is not the end of my story. Just the part where I learn who I am and care for it and love it into life.

I am the Prodigal Son, deciding to come home. I am the dark just before the dawn. This is the moment when the music swells and the hero stands up.

You are going to want to see this.
