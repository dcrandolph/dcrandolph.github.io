I heard a bird this morning. I was out in the cool, waiting for the Sun, waiting for something. I heard, but did not, could not, see the singer. I prefer the secret of it anyhow.

Some part of me used to want to see, would reach for a guidebook and a flashlight, and in so reaching would scare the bird away and miss the song. The part that names always loses.

What can we name the birds, sky, trees, seas, that will make any difference to them? The only part of the experience changed by the pure performance ego act of a label is the same part that does the labeling. The sea does not care what we call it.
It calls us. That is the relationship.

Nature, the world, the people around me, they all call out for something, but they do not ask for me to name them. They already are what they are. I think they ask to be seen and heard and, for once, not evaluated. Isn't that what I want, too?

My job is to hear the bird, and, if I listen well and pure and whole, to hear its name. To name the bird is to cage it. To hear its name is to set it free.
