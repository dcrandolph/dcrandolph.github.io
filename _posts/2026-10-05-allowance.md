How do you feel?

I care. I am listening. I want to know how you're doing and how I can help. But that wasn't the question. 

How do you feel?

I mean how does this mechanism of feeling work? Where does it come from for you? Where does it go?

Let's try different questions. What are you not allowed to be angry about? Who says, and when, and how?

What if that repression is what you feel as anxiety and fear and pain now?

Because I know you were not born with those feelings. I know you. You were born free and happy. You are made out of love, in love, from love, to love. You were born with a sense of justice that got paused, repressed, stifled, deferred, maybe bruised. Maybe that is what started the downward spiral, back when you were a child.

But you're not a kid any more. You have my permission, for whatever it's worth, to give yourself permission to expect love and to be angry when it is not allowed in.
