Waiting. Waiting for the bell, for recess, for the bus home, for dessert and birthdays and Christmas and Summer and Saturday morning cartoons. Waiting for graduation and a job and a career and vacations and coffee breaks and a chance to take a breath. Everybody's working for the weekend. Waiting for retirement and then what? If everything is practice and all that is rehearsed is waiting, what happens next?

Waiting for the end. Hungry for heaven. Oblivious, willfully perhaps, to the idea that a faith that requires an apocalypse in order to make sense doesn't make a lot of sense after all, not when every moment of life is a forever Now and all of Everything is always right here and nothing real and true ever ends. 

Every moment is miracle and every second is scripture. Waiting for God to show up is the only real sin, the sin of not seeing, the sin of going in order to stop, the sin of the Geographical Cure written on the soul.

No last chances or bucket lists if you are Forever. But if you are, if the important and real and true parts of you go on with the weight of infinity and all of it is practice, would you keep doing what you are doing right now, and in so doing cast the spell of ongoing?
