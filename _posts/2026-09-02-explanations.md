I want to need no introduction. I want to be simple and straightforward and easy and demand no explanation. I am tired of offering them. Maybe the Golden Rule applies, then.

Explain love or music to me. The act eliminates the best parts of the thing being explained, for palatability? It's a distillation, and truth is steam escaping in the process of making something suitable for consumption.

Never explain yourself, not to me. Leave the best parts where they are in the Mystery. I will do the same, to the best of my ability.

What do you know, just like you know you are alive? Where does your brain go, when you know you are in love?

Proof is dualism. It invites opposites and opposition. But when I know, I know there is only one.

Let's put a Potter Stewart definition in that place. There are ten thousand disguises, but one face. 

I want to know it when I see it.
