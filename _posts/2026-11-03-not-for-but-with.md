I cannot pray for you, any more than I can think for you, grow for you, live for you. You require no advocate, and I cannot get between you and Infinity. There are no sufficient credentials.

Prayer is not the words we say, any more than love is the poems we write or the songs we sing. This all lives somewhere else.

Nobody gets to speak for the Mystery that goes by so many names, none of them sufficient. The part that can be expressed in words is not important. The Divine part of the Divine is exactly what gets left out when It is translated and retransmitted.

This is all so intimate. When you go to a concert, music is happening all around you, but it is only happening for you, with you, in you, when you agree with it and dance.

So what _can_ I do? I can see you. I can see you like I think the Mystery beholds you. Growing, loved and loving, learning, free. I can refuse to curse you with thoughts and vision of limitation. I can hold you accountable to a triumphant standard. I can tell, and see, the Truth.

I can hold you and cry with you, laugh with you, dance with you and with the Music that is bigger than either of us, and beyond what one person can do for another. What can I do? I can show up.

So here I am.
