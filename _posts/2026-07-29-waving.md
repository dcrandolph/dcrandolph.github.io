Sit with me, here by the fire. The sun will set soon. Watch it disappear across the water. Can you see where it goes? Should we give chase?

There is light here, and warmth still, you know. These stars, this fire, you! Only sit with me, here in the light.

You cannot see all of the ocean. But there is this wave, this sound, this crash. There is this.

You cannot see all of the Mystery. You cannot serve all of it. But you can see, and serve, and love the people that the Mystery gives you. 

You have been in the sea all day, and even now, on the shore, you feel the ebb and flow. You carry it with you now. Carry on!
