The world is weird and wonderful, and you are perhaps the weirdest thing about it, certainly the most wonderful. You stick out in the best possible way. You do not have to conform -- we are all already the same atoms and cells, and, zooming out and looking down from space, the same astronomy and physics and thermodynamics.

And yet somewhere in the middle is a very special set of experiences, of manifestations of the Mystery, of stories that need to be shared. There is you, a strange beatific bewildered soul on a journey into, and carrying, forever.

Can you let yourself be known? It takes courage, but you have been so brave so many times before. It takes strength, but you have been so strong, child. 

It takes all kinds of things, but most of all, it takes you, just as you are. Can you let yourself be known? Can you stand?

Maybe somebody, maybe lots of somebodies, whole schools and churches and families of somebodies, told you to sit and stay seated. But that time is over now. They were scared, but you do not have to be, not anymore.

The pain you feel is the world, hungry for you. Somehow, in the middle of forever, there is no time to wait. The pressure you feel is life, anxious to dance to your song. Can you let yourself be known? Can you stand? Can you play?
