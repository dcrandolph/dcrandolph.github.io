**Life doesn't happen between your ears.** Lots of things happen that you did not cover in your daily affirmations. You can think about pink bicycles all day long and never see one, and that does not mean you are bad at thinking. It means that life happens someplace else.

**Experience is experienced on the basis of feeling of feelings.** You are going to see and hear and think and live more of what you feel most profoundly, to the degree that you feel it. The more passionate or intense you feel something, the more of that you are going to live through. That is why lovers love and haters hate, and why they keep on doing it forever.

It's also why **you are not allowed to hate your job**, not if you ever want to leave it. If it still stirs up something in you, it means that you still have something to learn from it, and probably something more to contribute. It means that you are not done, and so you can't go. If you hate it more, it will rule you more.

It means that **healing cannot happen unless and until we let go** of whatever it is that needs to be healed. It means that "manifestation" or "attraction", whatever those words actually mean, are far less important than *release*.

Does somebody owe you, after all you have done for them? **Where is the debt**, in them or in your heart? Where can you let go and get done and move on? Do they need to do something, or do you?

You know that **the root of the word "sacrifice" comes from the latin meaning "to make sacred"**, right? 

So **can you give** it, really give it, and let it be uplifted and healed and made holy? Or are you keeping score?

Exactly none of enlightenment or happiness or love is transactional. **So let go.**
