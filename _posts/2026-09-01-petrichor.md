I hear the first drops tap my window.  
Pennies and pebbles from a prospective suitor.  
This time I forget to resist the temptation to run outside  
To respond.

I am forgetting all kinds of things these days  
Finally.

There it is  
The smell of change  
Of life coming back after so long in hiding  
Of a cycle and a circle unbroken  
Of shared acknowledgement

Each drop is aimed  
Meant.  
Intended.  
Let me intend too.  
I am seen.  
Let me see too.

Something is happening.  
The signs are everywhere.  
Gathering  
Like this storm  
Heavy now.

Let me stay outside.  
I am tired of boundaries.  If the signs point to anything  
It is that the veil is sundered.

Let it be sundered in me, too.  
Let that be my petrichor prayer.
